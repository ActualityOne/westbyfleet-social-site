Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026. This explains what personal information West Byfleet Social Club collects through this website, why, and what rights you have over it.

Who we are

West Byfleet Social Club, 41 Station Approach, West Byfleet, Surrey, KT14 6NE. For anything relating to this policy, contact the secretary at secretary@westbyfleetsocial.com or 01932 346240.

What we collect and why

Enquiry & contact formName, email, phone, and anything you write to us, so we can reply to your membership or venue hire enquiry. Sent via Formspree and delivered to our inbox.
Admin diary loginCommittee members who manage the events diary sign in with an email and password via Supabase. We don't collect this from the public.
Cookie notice preferenceA note in your browser's local storage so we don't show the cookie notice again. No personal data leaves your device for this.

We do not run analytics or advertising cookies, and we do not sell or share personal information with third parties for marketing.

Third-party services this site uses

  • Formspree, to deliver contact form submissions to our email
  • Supabase, to store the events diary and handle admin login
  • Google Fonts, to load the typefaces used on this site
  • Facebook and Instagram, linked from our footer (their own privacy policies apply if you visit them)

How long we keep it

Enquiry messages are kept only as long as needed to respond to and, where relevant, fulfil a booking, then deleted. Admin accounts are removed when someone leaves the committee.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you, or ask us to stop using it. Email secretary@westbyfleetsocial.com and we'll respond within a month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
