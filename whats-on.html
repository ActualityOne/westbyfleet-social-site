What's On

Something happens most nights of the week. Members get first word on dates through our noticeboard and Facebook page, but here's the regular rhythm of the club.

Upcoming Dates

Specific dates from our diary, kept up to date by the committee.

Monday

Bingo night in the main bar. Eyes down from early evening, all welcome, cash prizes on the night.

Wednesday

Quiz night on the last Wednesday of the month. Teams of up to six, general knowledge rounds, and a prize for the winning table.

Friday & Saturday

Gin & Fizz offers behind the bar both nights, alongside our usual four-plus real ales. See the Friday evening menu for food.

Saturday

Saturday lunch served 12-2.30pm, from a cooked breakfast to scampi and chips. Full Saturday lunch menu here.

Sunday

Sunday roast served 1-3.30pm. Some dishes need pre-ordering, so check the Sunday roast menu ahead of your visit.

Coming Up

A few dates already on the diary. More get added through the year; ask at the bar or follow us on Facebook for the full list.

Live Music

Something Else Band

Saturday 3rd October, from 8.45pm. Members free, guests £5 on the door.

Live Music

Karl Lewis: "Almost" Olly

Saturday 17th October, from 8.45pm. An Olly Murs tribute first half, cover songs second half. Members free, guest tickets £6.

Open to the Public

Christmas Market

Sunday 6th December, 12-3pm. Local stalls, festive gifts and a raffle. Stall enquiries to kerrywbsc@gmail.com.

Live Music & Special Nights

Bands play our lit stage most months, ranging from tribute acts to local covers bands. Karaoke nights and a meat draw evening are regulars too, and we always go big for New Year's Eve.

Exact dates are posted on our noticeboard and Facebook page a few weeks ahead. Members hear first.

Games & Tables

Outside of scheduled nights, the games room is open to members whenever the club is.

Snooker

Two full-size tables in a dedicated room.

Pool

Match-play tables in the main bar.

Darts

Three championship boards with a club league.

Poker

A 10-seat table for regular poker nights.
