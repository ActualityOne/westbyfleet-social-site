Membership

A club run for the community, by the community, since long before "social club" needed explaining. Join for the price of a few rounds and get a second living room in West Byfleet.

What Membership Gets You

Membership is open to anyone in and around West Byfleet. There's no committee interview, no waiting list, just a short application and a modest annual fee that keeps the bar prices low and the building looked after.

  • Two full-size snooker tables and match-play pool, no booking needed
  • Three championship dart boards and a club league each season
  • Weekly bingo, a monthly quiz, and a 10-seat poker table
  • Live bands most months, plus karaoke and curry nights
  • Sky Sports on multiple screens and a projector for the big matches
  • Members' bar prices, lower than anywhere else on the high street
  • First refusal on tickets for our busiest events, like New Year's Eve

How to Join

1. Come Down

Visit us during opening hours and ask at the bar for a membership form, or request one through the contact page.

2. Get Proposed

New members are proposed and seconded by two existing members. Don't know anyone yet? Come to a quiz night and you soon will.

3. Pay Your Subs

Once approved by the committee, pay your annual subscription and you're in, with a card for members' bar prices.

Ask About Joining

Common Questions

How much does membership cost?

Subscriptions are kept deliberately low, in line with similar community clubs in Surrey. Ask at the bar or via our contact form for the current rate, which is reviewed annually by the committee.

Can visitors use the club without joining?

Signed-in guests of members are welcome, as are CAMRA card holders passing through. If you'd like to try the club before applying for membership, ask a member to sign you in or get in touch and we'll arrange a visit.

Is the club suitable for families?

Yes. The club is child-friendly during the day and for family events, with a garden and relaxed atmosphere, alongside its evening social club activities.

Do members get priority for hiring the function room?

Members receive priority booking and a discounted hire rate for the function room, on top of everything non-members can already book for their own events.
