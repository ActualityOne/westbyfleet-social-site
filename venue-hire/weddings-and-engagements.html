Wedding & Engagement Celebrations

A relaxed, affordable venue for evening wedding receptions and engagement parties in West Byfleet, without the hotel price tag.

Check Your Date

An Unpretentious Reception Space

Not every couple wants a marquee and a five-figure bill. Our function room suits an evening reception, an engagement party, or a post-wedding celebration for family who couldn't make the ceremony, with a private bar, dance floor and stage for a DJ or band.

Couples typically book the room for the evening, arrange their own decorations and playlist or DJ, and choose a buffet from our kitchen or their own caterer.

  • Private bar and dance floor for your evening reception
  • Space to bring your own decorations and styling
  • Buffet catering from our kitchen, or bring your own caterer
  • Free parking for guests travelling to West Byfleet

A Few Practical Notes

Ceremonies

We're not licensed for the legal ceremony itself, so this suits an evening reception or celebration after the formal part of the day.

Styling

Bring your own centrepieces, favours and signage. Ask about our set-up window when you book.

Guest Numbers

The room comfortably suits gatherings from an intimate engagement party up to a larger evening reception.

Wedding & Engagement Questions

Can we hold a full wedding ceremony at the club?

We are not licensed for wedding ceremonies, but we host evening receptions, engagement parties and post-wedding celebrations very well, once the formal ceremony has taken place elsewhere.

Can we decorate the room ourselves?

Yes. Couples are welcome to bring their own decorations, table settings and centrepieces, and we'll agree a set-up window with you ahead of the day.

Do you provide the evening buffet?

Our kitchen can prepare an evening buffet for your guests, or you're welcome to bring in outside catering if you have something specific in mind.
