Common Questions

Do we need to be members to hire the room?

No. The function room is available to the public for private hire. Members do receive a discount on standard rates, so it's worth mentioning if you're a member or thinking of joining.

Can we bring our own caterer or DJ?

Our kitchen can handle buffets and finger food for most events, and you're welcome to bring an outside caterer for anything more specific. You're free to book your own DJ, band or entertainment.

How far in advance should we book?

Popular dates, especially Saturdays and December, get booked up months ahead. For a wake or funeral gathering we understand timing is tighter, so get in touch as soon as you have a date and we'll do what we can.

Is there parking and step-free access?

Yes to both. There's free on-site parking and the venue is step-free throughout, which matters for older guests or family gatherings after a funeral service.
