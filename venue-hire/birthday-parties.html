Birthday Party Venue Hire

From a first birthday in the garden to an 80th with a live band, our function room is one of the most affordable birthday venues in West Byfleet.

Check Your Date

Built for Birthdays, Big and Small

Birthdays are the most common reason people hire our function room, and the room is set up to suit them: an open dance floor, a stage for a DJ or band, a private bar staffed for your whole booking, and tables for guests who'd rather sit and talk.

Milestone birthdays, 18ths, 21sts, 40ths, 50ths and beyond, tend to book the room in the evening with the bar open late. Younger children's parties usually book an afternoon slot, with access to the garden when the weather's kind.

  • Open floor for dancing, plus a stage for your DJ or band
  • Private, staffed bar for the length of your hire
  • Buffet and finger food options from our kitchen
  • Garden access for daytime and children's parties
  • Flexible finish times for evening bookings

Planning Your Party

Decorations

You're welcome to bring balloons, banners and table decorations to make the room your own. Ask us about set-up time before the party starts.

Music

Bring your own DJ or use one of ours, or plug in a playlist. The stage has power and space for equipment.

Food

Choose a buffet from our kitchen, bring in outside catering, or keep it simple with bar snacks. Let us know numbers in advance.

Birthday Party Questions

Can you host both adult and children's birthday parties?

Yes. We regularly host milestone adult birthdays in the evening and children's birthday parties during the day, including a garden for children to spill out into in good weather.

Is there space for a DJ and dancing?

Yes. The function room has an open floor and a stage, big enough for a DJ, dancing and a dedicated area for older guests to sit and talk.

How late can a birthday party run?

Hire hours are flexible and can extend beyond our normal opening hours for private bookings. Tell us your finish time when you enquire and we'll confirm whether it's possible for your date.

Ready to Book Your Birthday?

Send us the date, rough guest numbers and what time you'd like to start, and we'll reply with availability and a price.
