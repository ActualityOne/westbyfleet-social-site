Community & Corporate Events

Meetings, AGMs, quiz fundraisers and local group get-togethers, hosted at community-friendly rates in the heart of West Byfleet.

Ask About a Booking

A Practical Space for Local Groups

We already host committee meetings, society AGMs and fundraising quiz nights for groups across West Byfleet, so the room is set up to handle them well: flexible seating, a bar if your event calls for one, and a team who understands what a local group needs from a venue.

Small businesses and clubs also use the space for training sessions, away days and daytime meetings, away from a corporate hotel price tag.

  • Flexible seating, theatre-style, boardroom-style or informal
  • Bar available for evening events, or a dry daytime meeting
  • Community rates for local charities, societies and resident groups
  • Tea, coffee and catering available on request

Local Charities & Societies

Small Businesses

Training sessions, team meetings and small away days, without booking out a hotel conference suite.

Common Questions

Do you offer discounted rates for local charities and community groups?

Yes. As a community club ourselves, we offer community-friendly rates for local charities, societies and resident groups. Mention your group when you enquire.

Can you set up the room for a meeting or presentation?

We can arrange tables theatre-style, boardroom-style or in smaller groups depending on your event, and can provide a screen area for presentations.

Is daytime hire available for business meetings?

Yes, the function room can be hired during the day for meetings and training sessions, separate from our evening social club activities.

Tell us what your group needs and when, and we'll confirm availability and a community rate.
