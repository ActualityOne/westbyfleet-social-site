Funeral & Wake Gatherings

A quiet, private room in West Byfleet where family and friends can gather after a service, with catering and the practical details taken care of.

Speak to Us About a Date

A Calm Space to Gather

Arranging a funeral leaves little time to think about a venue. Our function room gives you a private, comfortable space close to West Byfleet station where mourners can gather, share a drink and a plate of food, and take as long as they need without being rushed.

The room sits apart from the main club bar, so your gathering has privacy even while the rest of the club continues as normal. We can lay tables for a sit-down tea or keep things informal, whichever suits the family.

  • Private room, separate from the main bar
  • Buffet, sandwiches or a full spread from our kitchen
  • Flexible timing to work around your service
  • Free on-site parking and step-free access throughout
  • Understanding staff who can accommodate short-notice bookings

What We Can Arrange

Catering

From tea, coffee and sandwiches to a full buffet, we'll put together something that fits your numbers and budget.

Timing

We can hold the room for the afternoon or evening following a service, with no pressure to rush guests away.

Space for Reflection

Quieter seating areas away from the main gathering give family members room to step aside when they need to.

Questions Families Ask Us

Can you arrange a wake at short notice?

We understand funeral arrangements often come together quickly. Call us as soon as you have a date and we will do everything we can to fit around it, even with limited notice.

Do you handle catering for a wake?

Yes. Our kitchen can put together a simple buffet, sandwiches and tea and coffee, or a more substantial spread, based on numbers and budget you're comfortable with.

Is the room private during a wake?

Yes. The function room is separate from the main club bar, so your gathering has its own private space, away from any other club activity.

We're Here to Help

Call us directly or send a message with your date and rough numbers, and we'll talk through what we can offer.
