About West Byfleet Social Club

A community club next to West Byfleet station, run by a volunteer committee for the benefit of its members and the wider village.

Our Story

West Byfleet Social Club has been a fixture of the village for generations, a place where neighbours became friends over a game of snooker or a Monday night bingo card. Like many social clubs, we've weathered changing times by staying true to what people actually want: an affordable, unpretentious place to spend an evening.

The club is owned by its members and run day to day by a volunteer committee. Profits go back into the building and the activities, not to outside shareholders, which is part of why membership and drinks stay so reasonably priced.

What We Stand For

Community First

We exist for the people who walk through the door, not for a franchise or a brand. Decisions are made by members, for members.

A Genuine Welcome

New members, visiting CAMRA card holders and families booking a wake or a party are all treated the same way: properly looked after.

Value for Money

From membership subs to bar prices to function room rates, we aim to be the fair, affordable option in West Byfleet.
