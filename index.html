More Than a Club, a Second Living Room

Tucked beside West Byfleet station, we've been the place locals come to unwind, compete and catch up for years. Membership is inexpensive, the welcome is genuine, and there's always something on.

Snooker & Pool

Two full-size snooker tables and match-play pool tables in a dedicated games room.

Darts

Three championship boards and a friendly club league throughout the season.

Bingo & Quiz

Weekly bingo and a monthly quiz night, an easy way to meet people and win a prize.

Live Music

Bands on our stage most months, plus karaoke and curry nights for something different.

Join a Club That Feels Like Home

Membership costs little more than a round of drinks and opens the door to every social night we run, discounted bar prices, and a genuine sense of belonging in West Byfleet. New members, and CAMRA card holders passing through, are always welcome.

  • Access to snooker, pool, darts and poker nights
  • Priority booking for special events and live bands
  • Discounted drinks and home-cooked bar snacks
  • A friendly, informal club run for the community, by the community

Hosting an Event? Hire Our Function Room

Away from club nights, our relaxed function room and private bar are available to hire for birthdays, weddings and engagement parties, christenings, wakes and memorial gatherings, and community or corporate get-togethers. No membership required to book.

  • Private bar and dance floor, licensed for your own hours
  • Buffets and finger food from our in-house kitchen
  • Rates well below hotels and hired halls nearby
  • Free on-site parking and step-free access, next to the station
